# Liverton Responsive Verification

The Liverton storefront was visually reviewed after the navigation, footer, banner, and environment-configuration updates at four target viewport classes: mobile (`390×844`), tablet (`768×1024`), laptop (`1024×768`), and desktop (`1440×900`). The reviewed routes were Home, Products, Contact, and Status where applicable.

The review confirmed that the mobile menu replaces the desktop navigation without overflow, product cards collapse from three columns to two and then one column, the Contact form stacks cleanly, the two-column footer becomes readable stacked content, and the dashboard banner remains inside the home content flow. Tablet and laptop layouts retain usable product grids and readable footer columns; desktop retains the full navigation and two-column footer.

## Banner source of truth

The home page intentionally contains two different promotional surfaces with different jobs. The slim top announcement bar is a site-wide status/announcement strip for short-lived operational or campaign messages. The `dashboard-banners` region is the single configurable media area for video, poster, announcement, and promotional content. The former duplicate lower dashboard-card strip has been removed, so configurable media is not duplicated elsewhere on the home page.

The configurable media region is managed in the separate Admin-Store application. Admin-Store uploads media through the signed Cloudinary flow, stores banner metadata and publication state in the shared backend, and the customer storefront reads only published records through `storefrontContent.publishedBanners`. No banner URLs, public IDs, or copy overrides are required in Vercel.

The screenshots used for this review are generated by the managed WebDev preview. After connecting Vercel, repeat the same route checks in a Preview deployment because external hosting configuration and environment values are deployment-specific.
